About Grid Animator

Created by Javier Reyes

What it does

Turn a pile of images into a moving grid for posts and reels. Pick a layout, shape the tiles, add a reveal and some motion, then save a PNG or record a looping video.

Everything runs in your browser. Your images are not uploaded anywhere.

Who I am

I'm Javier Reyes, a Senior Motion Designer and Art Director (RGD) with 15+ years of experience, based in Edmonton, Alberta. I help tech and fintech teams explain complex ideas through 2D and 3D motion, motion toolkits and product visuals.

I think in systems, and I build hyper-customized tools around how a specific person or team really works. It’s also my way of exploring a new frontier: every tool I build teaches me something new.
